Lunch Ordering Process: Before vs After

Tatay (food vendor) → office employees · Current manual flow vs proposed Lark form flow
Tatay (vendor) Employee HR Lark / System

BEFORE: Current Process (Manual)

Tatay walks department to department
Start (Morning)
Tatay
Writes the menu of the day by hand
Tatay
Goes to each department with the menu
Employee
Verbally tells Tatay the order Picks from the menu, or asks for extra food not on the menu
Tatay
Writes down each order and employee name Repeats for every department
Risks: mishearing, missed employees, no single record, lots of walking.
Tatay
Finishes all departments, prepares the food
Tatay
Returns at lunch time, packs food per employee Packages labeled with employee names
Employee
Receives own package
End

AFTER: New Process (Lark Form)

HR collects orders digitally; Tatay only reads a printout
Start (Morning)
Tatay
Gives the menu to HR
HR
Creates / updates the form in Lark Predefined food list from the repository
☐ Fish   ☐ Adobo   ☐ Menudo
[ new food name ] [Add]
Lark / System
"Add new" saves the food to the food repository Available for future days
HR
Ticks the foods Tatay is delivering today, then Submit
Lark / System
Publishes the employee form with only today's foods Open for 1 to 2 hours
Employee
Fills in the form, then Submit
Fish  [ 1 ]
Menudo [ 2 ]
Remarks: (extra food request)
Quantity field per food. Name is fetched automatically from the Lark account
HR
Clicks "Generate menu for that day"
Lark / System
Produces the summary
Top: per food
Total orders per foode.g. Menudo: 5
Bottom: per employee
Each name with their orderse.g. Emp 1: Menudo, Fish
HR
Prints the summary form and hands it to Tatay
Tatay
Prepares and delivers food as listed on the form
Benefits: no walking around, one record, auto names, clear totals.
End
